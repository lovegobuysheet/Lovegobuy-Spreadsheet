01 / Research with a purpose

Similar photos do not mean identical products.

A Lovegobuy spreadsheet is a shortlist of source links and order notes. When searching a catalogue or comparing visually similar listings, record what makes each option different: seller, materials described, measurements, variant and current availability.

FIND

Save a traceable source

Use the FashionReps Lovegobuy catalogue as a discovery starting point. Open the original listing and save its URL, seller and date checked. A catalogue entry is not an endorsement.

COMPARE

Write down the differences

Compare the actual size chart, option names, included accessories and seller descriptions. Do not assume a lower price refers to the same variant or bundle.

DECIDE

Resolve the next question

Assign one next action: confirm stock, request measurements, review photos or obtain a route quote. Keep uncertain rows on hold until the missing information is available.

02 / Warehouse review

Make your QC decision explicit.

Record the evidence for the item you actually ordered. A review of a similar item cannot replace checking your own warehouse photos. Mark the decision and its date so it remains clear which items are ready for consolidation.

QuestionEvidence to saveNext action if unclear

Are visible details acceptable?Photo references for seams, marks and fastenersAsk for a clearer view of the concern.

QC photos are limited evidence. They do not establish every material property, authenticity claim or long-term durability. Confirm the current return conditions directly with the service.

03 / Shipping comparison

Compare the quote, not just the route name.

Enter each available route into a separate row after obtaining a current quote. Air and sea options, where offered, can differ in eligibility, handling, time estimates and parcel limits. Only compare routes actually available for your destination and goods.

  • Record the packed dimensions, actual weight and the route's billable-weight rule.
  • Keep the quote date, currency, included charges and any extra fees together.
  • Check restricted items, delivery coverage, tracking and compensation conditions.
  • Treat transit times as estimates and allow for warehouse processing and customs.

Illustrative budget

An item subtotal of 80, domestic delivery of 5, fees of 4 and freight of 26 produces a planning subtotal of 115 in one chosen currency. Destination charges may be additional. These are example amounts, not Lovegobuy rates.

05 / Questions before checkout

Keep the decision grounded.

Is this the official Lovegobuy spreadsheet?

No. This independent guide helps organize your research. Visit Lovegobuy for current platform terms and account support.

What if two listings use the same photos?

Check the original sellers, descriptions, variants and measurements separately. Matching pictures do not prove matching goods.

Where can I find product links?

Browse the FashionReps Lovegobuy product catalogue, then verify the source listing, stock and selected variant before ordering.

Should I approve every item before combining a parcel?

Resolve open QC questions and record your keep or return decision before international dispatch. Confirm the agent's current process and deadlines.

Will this guide guarantee a delivery date?

No. Route availability, processing, customs and local delivery can change. Use a current quote and the applicable service terms.
